# Final scientific methods

The study evaluates 14 laboratory targets, each with two predictor sets. All models predict change from a held laboratory prior. Weather/intake inputs form the primary no-profiler analysis; the second set must retain at least one profiler channel. Model family, predictors and representation are selected independently inside each set. The fixed primary set is not replaced by whichever model scores best later.

Laboratory records use one consistent DL/2 substitution for explicitly qualified non-detects. Numeric zeros stay zero. Modelling timestamps follow the existing noon convention; actual collection/result-availability times are not established. Zero-horizon environmental inputs end at 11:00, so these are near-sampling estimates rather than verified operational forecasts.

Profiler sensor-range failures become missing; narrower subject-matter review flags remain available. Shallow observations lie within 2.94 +/- 0.40 m. Each cast supplies the closest recorded depth to 35 m within 30--40 m. Native sampling times and missing channels are retained; no vertical or temporal interpolation is applied.

Summary representations use 12/24-hour blocks or full-window means. One deeper observation supports mean/min/max; deeper SD uses at least two observations across the whole window and appears once. Other block statistics require two observations. Flattened inputs use the latest actual observation in each interval ending at that hour. The held prior occurs once per window and is available no later than its start, including at shifted horizons.

Weather corrections are chosen by 2020-fit/2021-validation RMSE. Temperature uses an offset; radiation, humidity and wind components use affine corrections; pressure and precipitation remain raw. Each fold fits corrections using all paired history since 16 January 2020 through its training cutoff, then holds them fixed on validation inputs. Station values take precedence and corrected MET Nordic fills gaps.

Nominal chronological membership is fixed before input-coverage filtering. Three expanding validation folds occupy the training period. The within-set objective is 1 - validation R-squared + 0.25 times the lost-validation fraction, requiring five available observations. Entirely unavailable folds count as lost coverage. Search uses 240 subsets, beam six, at most 60 swaps, a minimum of four predictors, and the leading four subsets plus the full offered pool for family/representation comparisons. XGBoost tuning has 250 trials; GP optimization has up to 250 epochs selected on independently prepared nested chronological folds. Stochastic finalists average seeds 0--5 only where every seed predicts; deterministic fits use one seed. After selection, each configuration is fitted on all eligible training-period observations, including earlier validation observations. During evaluation, earlier evaluation-period laboratory observations may supply priors and rolling references when they meet the information cutoffs; fitted model parameters stay fixed. Neither fitting choices nor candidate scores use final evaluation values.

Both sets receive the same six reference forecasts on shared dates: Training mean, Calendar, Calendar + prior, Persistence, Recent linear trend and Earlier-years seasonal mean. Training-dependent references use all eligible laboratory observations in the same nominal training period, not each model's smaller complete-input subset. Within validation, references use that fold's training history. Persistence uses exactly the model's shifted window-start prior. Recent linear trend may use fresher measurements, but none later than target minus (horizon + 1) hours. Seasonal uses all earlier calendar years, widening from +/-4 days to +/-15 days to all eligible earlier-year observations.

Primary scores use all available later observations for each no-profiler model. Added profiler value uses paired dates, with both models' complete coverage and actual fitted memberships reported. Matching evaluation dates does not equalize training histories or establish representativeness of missing periods. Negative R-squared is not evidence of constant or random predictions; nonpositive scores are summarized categorically in the main table, with exact numerical results supplied.

MAE/RMSE are in physical units. NRMSE divides RMSE by the scored target population SD. R-squared compares with the evaluation-period mean. Skill is 1 - model RMSE / lowest RMSE among the six references on the same observations. Nonnegative forecasts are floored after inverse scaling and level reconstruction, per seed before averaging; pH is unconstrained. Historical extrema are scaling parameters, not output caps.

Conditional 90% R-squared intervals use 2,000 whole-observation bootstrap resamples. Skill intervals use 2,000 circular observation-block resamples, with block length max(1,min(3,floor(n/4),n-1)). The exploratory support labels also use paired sign tests and fixed-prediction alignment tests. Profiler contrasts and GP-control contrasts use 4,000 paired circular resamples with blocks of three. These intervals do not repeat model selection or account for development decisions.

Total coliforms was chosen during exploratory development as the detailed case study because it had the clearest combination of predictive accuracy and reference improvement. There is no universal R-squared threshold for practical usefulness and no automatic numerical gate for this follow-up. Two fixed Matérn-5/2 GP controls use the held prior alone or the prior plus annual sine/cosine terms. Their optimization duration is chosen within training. No subset or family search is performed for them. Final primary Colony-count and Zinc MLR fits retain only the held prior after screening. Predictor summaries use fitted_features, not the candidate subset; the latter remains recorded separately in selected.json and the features field used to identify the selected configuration. They share the environmental GP's 112 fitted training and 50 evaluation observations.

Horizon fits retain the Total-coliform configuration and final fitting settings at shifts 0/12/24/48/96/144/168 hours. Both environmental windows and prior cutoffs move earlier; references are rebuilt from nominal training history. The zero-shift fit reproduces saved predictions and all seven fits predict the same 50 observations. This sensitivity does not establish operational warning time.

Ten Total-coliform block permutations move consecutive blocks of three laboratory observations across fixed dates; full within-set selection is repeated, and priors/responses/references are rebuilt. Both R-squared and improvement over each record's strongest reference are compared on matching dates. Changed evaluation variance can make a permuted raw RMSE smaller without better relative prediction. The experiment does not adjust for choosing this case after inspecting evaluation results; ten permutations cannot establish a conventional 5% permutation-test result.

Six affected primary configurations have DL/2 versus DL/sqrt(2) checks. Recorded fold/final settings, training memberships and prediction dates are retained; both responses and references are rebuilt. The DL/2 replay reproduces original predictions. Date influence separately omits one evaluation date from scoring fixed forecasts and reranks references, without refitting or choosing another arm.
